Leonida Ledger v2 — an offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. Every trophy carries its official name, description and grade, a guide in our own words with numbered steps, where and when to do it, a time estimate, and the named source each fact comes from.

What is inside:

- **Checklist tracker** — tick trophies off as you earn them; progress saves in the browser (localStorage), exports to a file and imports back. Filters by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, online), by grade, by search, plus hide-earned and a spoiler setting that masks hidden story trophies until they are earned.
- **Platinum roadmap** — five steps from the first mission to the platinum, with a platinum-at-a-glance card (1 platinum · 2 gold · 6 silver · 46 bronze, difficulty 4/10, about 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies) and the trophies of the selected step beside it, plus the 35-item 100% checklist behind A Long Road.
- **Missables & warnings** — the one possibly missable trophy (Positive Mental Attitude, with its full guide open beside it), what is safe to leave until later, and the five trophies whose trigger is still unconfirmed at launch (checked 19 Nov 2026 against PowerPyx, GTABase and PSNProfiles listings) — honestly marked, never guessed.
- **Cited guidance** — every guide names its sources (Exophase, PowerPyx, PSNProfiles, GTABase) beside the trophy, with the official list as the anchor.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — the guide is plain static files under `app/`, works with the network off, and makes no network requests.
